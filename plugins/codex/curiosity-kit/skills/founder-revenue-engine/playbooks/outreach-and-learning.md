# Outreach and learning

Use for bounded outreach drafts, reply handling, a small commercial experiment or analysis of supplied funnel results. This method does not need a sender, CRM, enrichment database or scheduler. External actions require an actual tool and the applicable user authorization.

## Draft a sequence that earns the next response

Use the chosen customer hypothesis, offer, source-linked context, approved proof, channel and intended response. If those are absent, identify the smallest missing decision or use the relevant local research/offer method. A broad “sell this to everyone” brief needs qualification, not a longer sequence.

For each proposed recipient or recipient type, preserve the source of personalization. Mention a relevant observed fact without pretending a relationship, using unrelated personal information or converting a business event into an invented pain. A funding announcement proves the announcement, not that the prospect needs the service. A research source does not establish consent.

Give each message one objective and one clear ask. A useful opener usually connects the source context to the job, states the offer plainly and asks a low-friction question. Under 90 words is a reasonable default for an opener, not a reason to omit a necessary qualification or override the user's format.

Produce complete drafts, not just fill-in templates, when the supplied information is sufficient. Missing personal detail stays omitted or a visible research placeholder. If the offer has no measured proof, propose a learning conversation, demonstration or bounded pilot rather than inventing a customer result. The sender must approve material terms and promises before any action.

Follow-ups should add something relevant: a genuine answer, available example, clarified scope or a different useful question. Do not repeat “bumping this,” manufacture an expiring opportunity or claim a report/sample already exists. Choose a finite sequence length and timing appropriate to the request, relationship and channel. Dates are proposals unless actually scheduled. No rule here enrolls anyone or waits/sends automatically.

Return each message with its channel, objective, source references and intended timing/trigger where useful. Separate the actual draft from rationale. Preserve any existing user-approved wording and explicit exclusions.

## Stop and respond to the actual reply

The proposed sequence stops on reply, opt-out, invalid contact, disqualification or its agreed cap. A reply requires reassessment rather than another scheduled pitch. Honour an opt-out across channels; switching from email to LinkedIn does not create permission to continue.

| Reply | Useful response decision |
|---|---|
| Interest or concrete question | Answer what was asked using supported information; propose the next step only if useful |
| Price/scope objection | Clarify the relevant deliverable, constraint or tradeoff; do not invent a discount or guarantee |
| “Later” | Preserve the stated timing; a future reminder/contact needs applicable authorization and actual setup |
| Referral | Treat it as a lead to assess, not blanket permission to contact or disclose the thread |
| Wrong person | Confirm the role gap; do not pretend the recipient is the buyer |
| Opt-out or invalid address | Stop; do not propose channel evasion or keep the address in a sequence |
| Ambiguous answer | Ask the smallest clarifying question, not a forced meeting request |

No observed reply means no inference that the recipient read, disliked or rejected the offer. A bounce, delivery record, open pixel and human reply are different observations. Do not alter domains, tracking, mailbox settings or account security to improve a metric without separately authorized work.

## Prepare one bounded commercial experiment

State the decision and current hypothesis, eligible segment, offer/message, channel, manual or supported delivery path, desired response, observation window, capacity and stop conditions. Compare one meaningful change at a time when there is a usable baseline. A changed audience plus changed offer plus changed message does not isolate which factor mattered.

Include the minimum useful assets and an observable learning criterion. “Prepare an offer brief and reviewable invitation” can be complete when those artifacts are ready. “Get five paying customers” is unfinished until actual evidence shows payment. A plan, prospect list or model simulation does not satisfy that outcome.

If the founder has two hours per week, design for that capacity. A manual conversation or narrow pilot may be the most informative next action. Do not add a CRM, ad funnel, instrumentation platform or autonomous loop when a short evidence note will do. If no real customer interaction is authorized, return the ready-to-review materials and the evidence collection plan without implying the experiment ran.

## Interpret results honestly

Before computing a rate, establish:

- Unit of analysis: unique recipient, company, opportunity, order or account.
- Cohort and date window, including elapsed time and maturity of opportunities.
- Eligible denominator and event definition.
- Source of each observation, duplicates, exclusions, missing data and status changes.
- Whether the offer was actually shown or made before judging conversion.

Separate delivered messages, any replies, positive replies, qualified conversations, offer exposure, commitments, paid pilots, revenue, refunds and repeat use. A single recipient replying twice is one recipient in a recipient-rate denominator. Negative replies and opt-outs are not positive interest. A meeting, an unsigned letter of intent and cleared payment are different commitments.

Do not add overlapping channel reports as unique outcomes. If one system claims twelve wins and another nine while the ledger has fourteen unique wins, report fourteen, explain overlap and preserve unknown attribution. A self-reported source is another fallible observation. Direct traffic alone does not prove top-of-funnel success.

Synthetic snapshot example: ten delivered recipients, three replies including one opt-out, two positive replies, one call, zero payments. Any-reply rate is 30%; positive-reply rate 20%; calls per delivered recipient 10%; paid outcomes per delivered recipient 0%. These are different metrics. If observation window, offer exposure and current opportunity status are missing, establish them before diagnosing why nobody paid. Zero so far is not evidence that the offer cannot work.

For money, retain currency, gross/net basis, recurring versus one-time period, discounts/refunds, and the definition of recognised or collected revenue supplied by the user. Do not combine incomparable periods/currencies or turn bookings into cash. State unresolved accounting definitions rather than give a false precise total. Small or selected samples do not establish causal lift, statistical superiority or a reliable forecast.

When data supports a decision, explain the actual implication: targeting, offer, proof, timing, delivery or message may need attention. A higher reply rate with fewer qualified prospects may be worse. Preserve contrary evidence and consider an unchanged or inconclusive result. Do not generate another hook merely because the first variant missed a commercial outcome.

## Return and recovery

Return the requested drafts, concise results or next experiment with sources and limits. Keep a small resume note only for work that will continue: what ran, exact artifact/version, permitted actions, current status, observed replies/opt-outs and next check. On uncertain send status, inspect the existing record before any authorized retry; do not duplicate communication to force completion.

Run the [customer-copy review](../references/evidence-and-review.md) on every message, including suggested replies. External operations stay pending when tools, authority or essential facts are missing. Report exactly what was prepared, what actually happened and what still requires action.
