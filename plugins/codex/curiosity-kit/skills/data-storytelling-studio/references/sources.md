# Source methods and attribution

This skill's prose, executable helper and fictional examples are locally authored. The following pinned methods inform the design; the package does not ship donor scripts, prompt bodies or model/controller settings. Source attribution is not proof of candidate or installed behavior.

## Original plugin functions

Earlier Codex and Claude Data Storytelling Studio editions supply the three core jobs: format/readiness selection, seven-dimensional chart-message audit, and answer-first executive readout. These operate as substantive local methods with full artifact production. Presence-only schema validation and cross-host companion probes are not semantic verification.

## Pstack / Potato

At [cursor/plugins 9f451cf875ad1239912762f67741e8e5ba6ac0f1](https://github.com/cursor/plugins/tree/9f451cf875ad1239912762f67741e8e5ba6ac0f1/pstack):

- [Why epistemics](https://github.com/cursor/plugins/blob/9f451cf875ad1239912762f67741e8e5ba6ac0f1/pstack/skills/why/references/epistemics.md): preserve support, inference, contradiction and unknowns through synthesis.
- [Benchmark Checklist](https://github.com/cursor/plugins/blob/9f451cf875ad1239912762f67741e8e5ba6ac0f1/pstack/skills/benchmark-checklist/SKILL.md): test the actual numerical sentence against the measurement. Performance-specific run counts are not generalized to business data.
- [Experience First](https://github.com/cursor/plugins/blob/9f451cf875ad1239912762f67741e8e5ba6ac0f1/pstack/skills/principle-experience-first/SKILL.md), [Technical Writing](https://github.com/cursor/plugins/blob/9f451cf875ad1239912762f67741e8e5ba6ac0f1/pstack/skills/technical-writing/SKILL.md) and [verification](https://github.com/cursor/plugins/blob/9f451cf875ad1239912762f67741e8e5ba6ac0f1/pstack/skills/create-verification-skill/SKILL.md): reader use, clear explanation and proof on the actual delivered form.

The source uses MIT, copyright 2026 Lauren Tan. Its controller, fixed models, broad autonomy, PR/schedule machinery and conversational pacing are not adopted. Original local domain prose adapts the ideas.

Official articles by Lauren Tan: [The Complete Guide to pstack Pt. 1](https://x.com/poteto/article/2094457600259842065) and [Pt. 2](https://x.com/poteto/article/2097732320606507506). Part 1 informs giving the agent tools to drive and observe the actual artifact, which here means running `scripts/chart.py` on real data and inspecting its parsed and full-width rendered output. Part 2 informs priming context and the reader's experience first, and answering an uncertain question with a quick prototype against real data. Neither requires more machinery, and this skill adds none. These are local applications of the methods; no fresh article access or reproduction of article text is claimed.
