# Visual

## Unit and bar

Choose a component, page section, or coherent asset family that can be rendered and compared. Freeze target viewports, content states, and export sizes. A whole site often needs several units; a card family needs a set-level check as well as individual checks.

Store actual reference images, with origin, capture conditions, and applicable unit. A website name or description of a screenshot is insufficient. Declare hard palette, typography, spacing, and accessibility requirements separately from craft judgments. Use only constraints approved for this project.

## Inspection recipe

Build the candidate and capture current pixels at every declared viewport and state. Record the actual commands and output hashes with `python3 scripts/inspection.py check --input payload.json`. The command runs an authorized local argv and reports execution evidence; it does not decide whether pixels satisfy the brief. Inspect outputs directly. A build failure, missing viewport, blank capture, or obsolete screenshot blocks quality judgment.

Pair comparable crops, content categories, resolutions, and viewport sizes. Hide filenames and removable metadata, then seal the identity map outside the critic's readable packet. Record visible provenance that cannot be concealed. Ask for the largest actionable gap in hierarchy, alignment, density, spacing, or contrast. "More premium" is not a repair instruction.

## Worked check and integrity

A pricing section must survive both the approved wide and narrow layouts with realistic content. Winning at the wide layout does not excuse clipped prices at the narrow layout. A social-card family must retain its hierarchy across all declared templates.

Verify current-build provenance, plausible image dimensions, loaded fonts and assets, and absence of placeholder copy or broken images. Check hard constraints independently of visual preference. Judge interactions and data behavior with the code adapter; screenshots cannot establish that a purchase button works. Use deck for slide-order arguments and brand for identity consistency across channels.
