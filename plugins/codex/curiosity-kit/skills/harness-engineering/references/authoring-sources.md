# Authoring and evaluation sources

This reference records method provenance for the expanded builder. Sources are benchmarks and design inputs, not runtime dependencies or authority to execute their tools. The skill owns its required methods locally. Source inspection, deterministic checks, cloud exercise and native qualification remain separate evidence.

## Preserved foundation

The existing Harness Engineering method includes thirteen original Codex skills and Claude's additional Context Doctor, along with full interview, architecture, file-operation, verification, maintenance, prompt-governance and Unslop resources. Its existing license terms and third-party notices remain bundled. Private baseline identifiers and qualification records are excluded from this distribution; no historical acceptance is inherited by a new installation.

The expansion rewrites three additional source capabilities into local methods: Skill Creator Pro's discovery/design/execution/grading/review/packaging, Skill Eval Loop's suites/baselines/regression/staged repair, and Harness Meta Audit's fourteen diagnostic lenses. The rest of Capability Operator remains a separate owner. Private Codex and Claude source revisions remain with their source records rather than in this distribution. Included owner-original contributions are licensed under MIT as described in [NOTICE](../NOTICE.md); preserve the separate terms of third-party material and do not disclose private source records.

## Applied experience

The builder retains source-derived lessons without inheriting private historical acceptance: inspect the real authenticated catalog; bind evidence to final artifacts; preserve source uncertainty and exact requested counts; keep helpers' structural claims narrow; make scratch/spill permissions explicit; use persistent launchers where needed; never refund interrupted runs; preserve raw failures and assistance; distinguish hash presence from equality; and repair affected work without a redundant campaign.

Source names, historical outcomes and quoted examples never grant new permissions. Required skill behavior is defined by the current request and local methods, subject to the host's actual authority rules.
