# Reporting, creator and audiovisual sources

Use [the evidence contract](evidence-contract.md) for dates, source bodies, locators, claim support and privacy. Available native search, supported reads, permitted browsing and user-supplied transcripts are first-class routes. These profiles do not install downloaders, promise provider access, unlock paywalls or authorize uploading audio.

## News and newsletters

Search the resolved topic and interval through relevant outlets and original publishers. Open the full accessible article and follow its named evidence. Preserve author, publication, update and event dates separately. Trace syndicated copies to their origin, and identify genuinely new reporting before counting another outlet as corroboration. A fresh repost of an old article stays old; a recent retrospective is recent discussion of an older event.

For newsletters, distinguish publicly accessible full text, preview/snippet, publisher metadata and paid-only content. Read the original article if access is already permitted. If only metadata is available, use it to nominate a source and label the body unread. Do not infer the paid article from a summary or imply a subscription exists. Verify any constructed or redirected publisher URL before citing it.

Techmeme can identify a story cluster and related coverage. Inspect the original linked articles, their dates and their independent contribution. An undated aggregator headline is a lead; an archive entry's date need not establish the underlying article's original publication.

Digg or similar curated clusters can identify active discussion and original social posts. Retain cluster rank/author/post counts as aggregator observations. Follow the original post permalinks and read them before using their claims. The curator TLDR, linked post and article may all represent one origin. Coarse "days ago" ages are approximate until checked, and a feed's fixed trailing period cannot replace a requested historical interval.

DripStack or similar newsletter indexes can provide public metadata and discovery leads when accessible. Verify the canonical publisher destination and available date, then open permitted original content. Index summaries and paid selections do not establish access to paid bodies. If the index is unavailable, use native publisher/topic search and record the missing lane.

## YouTube, podcasts and long-form video

1. Search the topic and verified creator/channel separately when both matter. Use the original title/topic, a shorter use-case query and relevant vocabulary variants as needed. Validate exact channel identity, video publication date and both interval bounds. A channel page or highly viewed old video does not fill a requested recent-video gap.
2. Choose enrichment candidates by relevance, in-window eligibility and expected evidence value before views. Set a bounded attempt budget, counting failed reads as attempts. A deep request can justify more transcripts and contrary voices; quick work still needs a readable basis for any spoken claim it makes.
3. Open the video/podcast page, description and available chapters. Keep video ID, creator, original URL, publication date, duration and observed metrics. Descriptions establish what the uploader wrote; they are not spoken transcripts. A title cannot supply a quotation.
4. Prefer the platform's accessible transcript/caption tracks or a supplied transcript. Read the relevant segment with surrounding context, and retain timestamp/chapter, speaker and language where available. Preserve human-caption versus auto-caption or transcription provenance. If translated, retain that fact and check wording before quoting. A missing timecode is a limitation, not a reason to invent one.
5. If captions are unavailable, distinguish confirmed no-caption content from blocked/player-error/unknown access. Try another permitted native read or use a supplied transcript. An already authorized transcription tool can be used only within its actual media access, upload, cost and execution permissions. A missing downloader/binary does not invalidate an available native transcript route. Do not install yt-dlp/ffmpeg, activate SSH or send private audio to an external provider by implication.
6. If authorized transcription produces chunks, preserve per-chunk provider/method, order, time offsets and missing/failed sections. A partial transcript remains partial. Check upload limits before transmission; failed segmentation is not permission to upload an oversized original. Do not treat an empty transcript as successful speech evidence or hide gaps when joining chunks.
7. Inspect comments when the research question concerns reception, corrections or reported replication. Read parent context and meaningful replies; retain author/date/permalink or a stable video-and-comment locator. Separate comments from the speaker's statements. A clean accessible page with no comments is different from a failed comment retrieval.

Quote only material actually read and within applicable limits. Extract themes in your own words with segment links rather than reproducing entire transcripts. An excerpted transcript may support a narrow claim while leaving the whole talk unreviewed. Record that boundary.

## Instagram and TikTok

Use distinct keyword/topic, hashtag and verified creator/profile lanes when relevant and available. A creator lane answers what that creator published; hashtag results are a broader and often noisier sample. Verify the specific account, post/reel/video URL and publication date for every retained item, including profile/hashtag results that a search filter may not cover.

Read the caption/description, accessible speech transcript and relevant on-screen content as separate evidence types. Preserve the method used to inspect speech or images. If only the written caption is accessible, do not describe it as the creator's spoken explanation. Transcript segments represented as structured objects must be read as their actual text/time fields rather than treated as a stringified transcript.

Prioritize substantive fit and date before views. For reception, inspect meaningful comments and context rather than only the most-liked replies. Retain observed views/likes/comments/shares, author, hashtags and duration when available; metric omissions remain unknown. No accessible in-window results means a thin/empty result, not permission to retain old viral clips as recent. Native public discovery does not guarantee full platform search, private profiles or provider-volume comment access.

## Pinterest and Xiaohongshu

For Pinterest, distinguish the pin's own permalink, board/creator and outbound destination. Read the linked original when factual or product details depend on it. Saves and board inclusion describe attention/curation, not validation. If no pin publication date can be established, keep it as undated inspiration/background rather than last-30-days evidence.

For Xiaohongshu, search relevant native-language topic/category and creator terms where supported, preserving exact identity and translation context. Inspect note text, date, media and relevant comments through an already permitted route. Record observed metric units carefully, including Chinese 万/亿 abbreviations. Do not invent exact counts from a rounded display. Keep any access-bearing query token out of public output; verify a safe stable permalink or state the citation limitation. A separately logged-in third-party service, new credentials or persistent installation is an external dependency, not an assumed fallback. A provider's one-week/half-year preset cannot establish the user's exact interval.

## AI-generated search/research summaries

An available search assistant, including Perplexity-style synthesis, can nominate sources. Its generated narrative is derivative, even when it includes citations. Open the cited originals, verify publication dates and support, and keep the synthesis distinct from independently read source items. Do not assign an undated synthesis the requested interval end as its publication date. If only citation metadata is accessible, limit the conclusion accordingly.

Use only an actually available, authorized route; no API key acquisition or paid deep-research job is required for this skill. If the user explicitly authorizes an available asynchronous research service, preserve the real request ID, status, timeout/error and usage information. A local timeout leaves the remote job pending unless completion/cancellation is verified. Do not resubmit a duplicate job or report an empty successful result because polling failed. Resume or report the exact remaining dependency within the original authority. Native search and manual original-source reading remain usable when such a service is absent.

## Check the final media claim

For every material statement, ask what was actually read: original reporting, transcript, caption, visible media, comment, metadata or generated summary. Attach the correct locator and date to that evidence type. Preserve sponsor/affiliate relationships, source lineage, missing sections and consequential disagreement. A public-only export cannot retain a private transcript-derived conclusion under an unrelated public article citation.
