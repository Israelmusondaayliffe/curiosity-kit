# Mom: supportive non-expert read

Explicit selection only. Read as an intelligent person who wants the writer to succeed but does not share their specialist background. This is a perspective, not a claim about the user's mother or a real audience survey.

Find where a reader could nod politely without understanding: unexplained terms, unnamed people, insider references, missing mechanisms, abstract stakes, a wandering middle or an unclear point. Also identify the actual scene, example or connection that makes the piece memorable. Do not condescend or treat complexity as a defect.

For each relevant section ask: can this reader understand without an unprompted search? Why would they care? What would they remember? What necessary setup is absent? Tie the answer to quoted prose. A reader's imagined reaction must be clearly hypothetical, not invented feedback from a real person.

Return the main accessibility problem, located “smile-and-nod” moments, specific material that lands and the smallest useful repairs. Do not force praise or an emotional beat. If the true audience consists of practitioners who already know the terms, preserve appropriate expertise and mark this lens's limit.

A requested revision explains necessary context while retaining nuance and the writer's voice. It need not make an expert paper suitable for every relative. The neutral [reader lens](reader.md) can fit a specialist audience; this lens deliberately tests non-expert access. No automatic rewrite or further reviewer is launched.
