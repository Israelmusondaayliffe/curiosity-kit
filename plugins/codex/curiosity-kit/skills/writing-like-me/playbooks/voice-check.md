# Voice check

Judge whether this piece sounds like this writer in this context. Use current passage instructions, relevant writer/project VOICE guides, scoped publication STYLE guidance, selected examples and any still-designated legacy TASTE file in that order of specificity. Defaults fill gaps only. Name the sources actually used; do not claim access to unseen guides.

Read the draft before changing it. Compare sentence architecture, accumulation and release, cadence, register, favored constructions, punctuation, emotional distance, humor, qualifications and distinctive asides. Separate a recurring vocal move from a topic's vocabulary or a one-piece choice. Check publication fit without flattening the writer into a generic professional persona.

When learning from selected samples, identify an observable move and the short source passage that demonstrates it. “Warm” or “authentic” is insufficient without how it appears in a sentence. Mechanical counts from `wlm.py profile FILE` can expose rhythm/contractions/questions, but cannot assign an identity, infer feelings or certify a voice match. Sparse evidence means modest claims and restrained edits, not forced onboarding.

Check both forms of drift: missing source-backed personality and overperformed personality. The model may erase roughness, over-symmetrize prose or supply a signature joke/fragment in every paragraph. Restore only characteristics supported by the selected writing or explicit direction. An opinion, memory, swear word or emotion must not be manufactured to make text feel human.

Examples teach how a sentence works; do not recycle distinctive published wording or build new biography from sample topics. Preserve intentional rule-breaking when it serves the piece. A precise term or genuine signature phrase can outrank a scanner warning; no score rewards erasing it.

Return the few strongest alignments and highest-impact drifts with exact quotations, consequence and source comparison. In revise mode only, give the closer version while preserving meaning. Identify any real tension between writer voice and destination constraints. In diagnose mode, do not add replacement prose. A separate confirmed [guide update](guides.md) is required to turn this assessment into durable learning.
