# Final readiness

Assess the requested artifact against its brief, audience, sources and active guides. This is one bounded assessment, not a restart of all drafting stages. A personal reflection need not prove a business thesis; an informative post need not have suspense, transformation or a CTA.

Check purpose, completeness, factual scope, attribution, protected material and recognizable voice. Inspect typos/formatting where they affect the requested standard. For a substantial piece, skim headline, opening sentences, subheads, section openings and ending; identify a misleading skim impression or context missing at the point of need. Read a short post in full. When useful, compare a twenty-second account of the delivered piece with its opening promise and locate the smallest repair.

Return ready, almost-ready or needs-work with material findings and enough location/evidence to act. Required source support or independent review that is unavailable remains unresolved. A quiet scanner or a completed checklist is not a quality verdict. No numerical score or invented strength is necessary.

In diagnose mode leave the piece unchanged. When revision is already authorized, repair warranted issues directly and check the affected passages once. Preserve meaning, useful detail, uncertainty and source-backed personality. Ask only about a consequential writer choice. Apply one final Unslop DELIVERY check to the final authored result, not a chain of full rewrites.

The piece is ready only within the tested/requested scope. Publication, sending, scheduling and file moves need their own authority. Do not claim a source was verified, a reviewer ran or an action succeeded without actual evidence.
