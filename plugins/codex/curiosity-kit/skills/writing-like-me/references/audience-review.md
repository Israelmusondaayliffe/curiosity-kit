# Audience-calibrated review

Use within the requested review, not as an automatic extra panel. Pick the audience that actually matters. Source truth and protected material apply to every audience; do not impose business decision criteria on a personal essay.

- Learner: inspect hidden prerequisites, unexplained terms, difficulty jumps, missing steps and a usable progression. Respect intelligence; avoid “obviously/just” where it conceals a hard step. Add only sourced or clearly hypothetical examples and needed why-before-how context.
- Executive: can the reader identify the real decision, finding or consequence quickly? Inspect evidence, alternatives, constraints, timing and risk. Quantify only what sources support. A status note may need no recommendation or ask.
- Technical expert: examine definitions, mechanisms, implementation reality, edge cases, compatibility, failure behavior and measured claims. Do not replace precision with marketing or invent benchmark numbers.
- Skeptical peer: identify assumptions, alternative explanations, scope, evidence quality and serious counterarguments. Distinguish correlation from causation, consensus from evidence and emotional appeal from support. Strengthen, narrow or remove only within the writer's accepted position.
- General reader: inspect insider references, needed orientation, pace, concrete value and the actual writer's voice. A shorter text that hides the mechanism is not clearer.
- Internal team: test whether ownership, sequence, exceptions, constraints and rationale make the document usable in real work. Do not invent a responsible person or deadline.

Prioritize flaws that defeat the purpose, then credibility/support, then major comprehension problems, then polish. Quote the actual evidence and name the consequence. No minimum number of weaknesses or compulsory score applies. Use more than one audience only when the brief calls for it; explicit expanded panels follow their own bounded method. After an authorized repair, check the affected issue and any introduced claim once.
