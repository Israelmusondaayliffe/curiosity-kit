# Outline working shape

Use only applicable prompts. These are not required output headings or fixed beat counts.

## Opening
Main contribution; opening move; supplied beats; governing claim or live question; honest reader promise.

## Body section
Main contribution; relationship to previous section; actual sequence of scenes/claims/questions/evidence; source locators; what is established or remains uncertain.

Repeat for the sections the material needs.

## Ending
What the body earns; changed decision, consequence, image, qualification or unresolved question; connection from the final section.

## Open loops
Missing support; consequential writer choice; competing interpretation; transition or example still needed.
