# Method provenance and adaptation

LoopKit adapts earlier Codex and Claude editions. This source summary establishes attribution, not fresh execution or acceptance evidence.

Original Codex and Claude LoopKit supply six functions: bounded contract design, execution, fresh verification, interruption recovery, truthful scheduling and diagnosis. Their state/core implementation is rebuilt rather than wrapped. Caller-supplied machine-pass booleans become captured process results; the authoritative snapshot replaces separate logical state commits. Old receipts remain historical evidence, not imported proof. Original automatic Goal creation and stale host/product assumptions are deliberately removed.

Pstack capability source: `cursor/plugins` revision `e43c7ee26e0038c6c1fa8380dd34ce86ff94cb2a`. Adapted methods include direct artifact proof, reusable real-surface verification, serial verifiable units, safe pause/context preservation, bounded ownership and requested reflection with structural corrections. The large orchestrator's agent fleet, stack tooling and persistent service machinery are not necessary for this serial unit runtime.

Matt Pocock capability source: `mattpocock/skills` revision `24fe0ef7737efae15c87225755e9f6f5965e4888`. Adapted methods include a symptom-specific red-capable feedback loop, minimized reproduction, falsifiable hypotheses, targeted probes, regression checks, and requested retrospective improvements. Provider-specific invocation assumptions and mandatory sibling skills are replaced by local methods and supported host capabilities.

All adaptations remain subject to matched per-host acceptance tests. Mapping a source to a destination establishes accounting, not parity or superiority.
