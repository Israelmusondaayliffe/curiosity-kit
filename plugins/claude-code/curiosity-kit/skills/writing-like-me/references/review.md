# Two independent writing reviews

The parent writer owns review for a complete post, article or substantial deliverable. Unless the user skips it, obtain two separate reviews against the same frozen draft and relevant brief, evidence and voice/style guidance. Tiny isolated edits and incidental replies do not launch reviewers. Child craft methods never launch more reviewers.

1. Unslop/voice/readability reviewer: read the local Unslop and voice-check methods in diagnose mode. Identify generic phrasing, unsupported connective tissue, performed personality, revision residue, lost voice and missing explanation. Preserve useful detail and warranted uncertainty.
2. Quality/value/context/intent/support reviewer: read developmental-edit and business-and-claims as relevant. Check the requested deliverable, audience, purpose, factual support, argument and reader payoff, including reasons, mechanisms or general premises added in the writer's voice that the sources do not contain. Do not demand a thesis, drama or call to action from a piece that needs none.

Both return material findings with exact locations, evidence, consequence and a remedy. No defect quota or compulsory numerical score. Neither edits the shared draft, sees the other report first, saves lessons or starts children. A sound draft may yield no material findings.

Use actual independent workers supported and permitted by the selected host. Respect the selected model; otherwise choose a capable available model suitable for judgment and required tools, considering cost. Do not pin a model/version, invent an agent selector or silently substitute a weaker reviewer. Model metadata is recorded when exposed, never guessed. Native Claude does not consume Codex openai.yaml as an activation contract.

If no independent mechanism is available or authorization forbids it, say which missing or failed review is unavailable. A missing or failed review cannot pass. A writer's own pass may improve the piece but must be called self-review. Deliver useful authorized work with that limitation rather than fabricating reviewers or passing missing checks. If independent review is an explicit acceptance requirement, readiness remains unresolved.

The writer accepts findings supported by the brief, sources or concrete reader needs, and rejects blanket taste changes. Integrate once, protect exact material, check newly introduced claims and recheck an identified repair once if warranted. Do not restart a panel by habit. Expanded panels, named creative lenses and debates require explicit selection and a finite agreed scope.
