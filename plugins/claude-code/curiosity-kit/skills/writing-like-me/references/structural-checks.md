# Structural and source-aware cleanup

Use when notes, transcripts or earlier drafts exist, or when polished prose still feels generic. Coherence is not a defect. The question is whether organization, certainty or personality outruns the supplied material. When sources are absent, make conditional observations rather than factual accusations.

## Eleven structural checks

1. Unsupported connective tissue: trace motives, feelings, memories, scenes, sequences, causal bridges and outcomes to supplied evidence. Plausibility is not provenance. Remove the addition, mark an inference or ask the writer.
2. Epistemic inflation: compare observation versus intent, suspicion versus diagnosis, possibility versus mechanism, partial test versus general finding and experiment versus transformation. Restore the source's actual uncertainty.
3. Causal closure: does one cause conveniently explain every example? Did chronology become a deliberate strategy? Does a tidy conclusion erase mixed evidence? Separate event from interpretation and let exceptions remain.
4. Framework compliance: equal-sized sections, uniform mini-lessons, padded lists or a complete taxonomy may serve a template rather than the material. Reorganize by actual evidence, discovery or decision. Do not add random disorder as camouflage.
5. Performed voice: excessive jokes, fragments, metaphors, coinages or register clashes may imitate a recognizable move too densely. Retain source-backed oddity; cut synthetic personality. No paragraph needs a mandatory voice flourish.
6. Metaphor infrastructure: test whether headings, transitions and callbacks extend an image after its explanatory work ends. Keep each return only when it adds understanding, not literary symmetry.
7. Portable profundity: could the sentence move unchanged into unrelated copy? Reattach it to a named person, event, choice or consequence. If specificity removes its force, the shape may have been doing the work.
8. Reader assignment: a personal example does not establish what “you” have felt or what “we” all do. Keep the claim with the actual observer or offer recognition without assigning experience.
9. Decorative research: does a source answer or complicate a live question, or merely certify a predetermined conclusion? Name its finding and relevant limit, explain what it changes, and remove unsupported borrowed authority.
10. Over-resolved ending: check for an unearned decision, transformation, universal lesson, recap or ceremonial callback. End on the strongest supported consequence, cost, concrete next step or specific unresolved question.
11. Argument self-commentary: distinguish making an argument from grading or announcing it. Evidence self-rating, structural announcements, empty labels of uncertainty and interpretation before a quote can delay substance. The deletion test asks what necessary meaning disappears. The referent test asks whether the sentence points at the piece rather than its subject. Preserve actual uncertainty labels, navigation and adjudication when the reader needs them.

## Sentence-level checks

After source/structure, examine generic frames, inflated vocabulary, corporate abstractions, stock emphasis, empty imagery, reflexive hedging, formal connectors, vague authority, repeated contrast formulas, forced triplets, uniform rhythm, slogan-like equations and recap endings. [Pattern catalog](pattern-catalog.md) names all inherited families. Do not let word matching outrank an actual source comparison.

For imagery use six tests: deletion (what meaning is lost?), mapping (what mechanism corresponds?), swap (could any topic fill it?), restatement (does it only repeat nearby prose?), payoff (what inference or felt understanding is added?), callback (does returning to it still help?). Keep tactile or fresh imagery when it carries the writer's point of view or makes a mechanism graspable. A metaphor is not evidence.

Use specificity checks: who acts, what happens, what the reader should understand/do, and which actual person/system/event is meant. When the source supplies no concrete replacement, qualify or cut; never fill the gap with invented specificity.

## Repair order and recovery

Restore source support and certainty, reopen false closure, let material determine structure, remove useless self-commentary, reduce performed voice, correct reader/research overreach, then edit vocabulary and rhythm. Preserve explanations an actual reader needs.

If editing strips voice, restore characteristic phrasing and cadence from the selected source. If it becomes forced-casual, revert decorative changes. If a factual detail changed, recover it from evidence rather than memory. If exact material moved or changed, inspect the baseline and protected comparison. Recheck the concrete repair once; stop circular polish and return the useful result with honest limits.
