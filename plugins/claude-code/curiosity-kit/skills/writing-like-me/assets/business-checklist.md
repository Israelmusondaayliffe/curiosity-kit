# Business writing checks

Use only questions relevant to the requested artifact.
- Can the intended reader identify the purpose and use the information?
- Does each material claim have support at its actual scope?
- Are the mechanism, tradeoffs and uncertainty understandable?
- Are ownership, dates and commitments supplied rather than invented?
- Does the format serve the purpose without compulsory sections or a sales pitch?
- Does a proposed next step reflect the real request and authority?
