# Evidence and commercial review

Use this reference before qualification, recommendation or final commercial copy. It supplies local review expertise; no external writing/review skill is required.

## Keep evidence usable

For a material observation, keep a stable source ID and canonical URL or supplied-record locator, entity/author, publication/event date where known, observation/as-of date, access status and the specific supported fact. A locator must let a reader find the actual evidence. Never invent a URL, quote, person or timestamp to fill a field.

Separate direct observation, a supported report, inference, hypothesis and unknown. These need not become five headings on every answer. Their distinction must survive the reasoning and output. A customer's quote is evidence of what that customer said; it is not automatically a market-wide fact. A vendor's pricing page proves an advertised offer, not realised sales or the customer's spend.

Unknown-date sources can inform background. They cannot establish current-window demand without dated corroboration. Preserve historical evidence when it matters and explain why; don't quietly discard contrary findings to make the current story cleaner. If an old positive statement conflicts with a recent cancellation, retain both with their dates and changed context.

Mark unread/snippet-only/inaccessible sources. A search result can suggest a page to read; it does not establish full-page review. “Unavailable” differs from “no results.” Do not hide source-quality warnings in HTML or claim exhaustive coverage from a restricted set.

Link derived claims to evidence. A repeated article, quote or copied post is not another independent customer. Keep source diversity, author concentration and uncertainty visible. When a record mixes observed and inferred material, separate the two rather than giving the whole record a stronger label.

## Judge evidence against the requested job

For an ICP, ask whether pain, situation, user/buyer, trigger, alternative, authority and disqualifiers are supported or labelled hypotheses. For a prospect, require meaningful evidence of pain, need or timing, not only an industry match. A score can order candidates but cannot erase disqualification or make an unknown factual.

For an offer, distinguish deliverable acceptance from a customer outcome and a market claim. Capacity, costs, exclusions and buyer responsibilities matter. Do not call a founder-selected price validated without appropriate evidence. Praise, replies, calls, pilots, payments and retention support different conclusions.

For comparisons, preserve the main entity and a fair scope/window. A source access failure is not inferior quality. For hiring, a verified role posting may suggest investment but cannot establish a specific roadmap or budget. For learning, define denominator, window, maturity, offer exposure and unique event identity before interpreting rates.

## Review every line the user might repeat

Check the actual customer message, post, caption, opener, subject line, CTA and suggested reply, not just the surrounding analysis. A cautious report can still contain an unsupported sales sentence.

- Trace facts, numbers, quotations, first-person experience, opinions, emotions and outcomes to the allowed evidence or supplied voice.
- Preserve exact counts, units, ranges, dates, negatives and conditions. “At most three attempts in total” does not mean “three retries.” A single example cannot become “most customers.”
- Do not invent ongoing investigations, service actions, follow-ups, refunds, guarantees, deadlines, scarcity, relationships, availability or what an end user saw. Leave a fact the sender must supply as a clear placeholder; do not disguise it as ready-to-send fact.
- Distinguish sequence from causation. A result after a change may be consistent with the hypothesis without proving the change caused it.
- Verify that hook and body make the same bounded promise. A compelling headline must not outrun the proof.
- Mark synthetic examples and proposed outcomes. They cannot serve as testimonials or measured results elsewhere in the artifact.
- Ask whether the recommendation fits this buyer, stage, capacity and constraint. A rule of thumb is not a universal business law.

Revise unsupported text, narrow the claim or state the missing evidence. Do not compensate with a disclaimer after an unequivocal false promise. If the requested premise is unsupported, say what the evidence can support and provide that useful alternative without pretending it meets the original unsupported claim.

## Edit plainly without fabricating a person

Use concrete words, meaningful paragraphs and an appropriate reader sequence. Preserve precise terminology. Remove generic puffery, vague attribution, empty novelty and unsupported certainty. Keep warranted uncertainty.

Do not reward random numbers, lowercase, fragments, profanity or false imperfections as signs of authenticity. Preserve those choices only when they belong to the supplied writer or explicit request. A writing-pattern detector is neither authorship detection nor a truth judge. No synthetic score or “clean” badge establishes credibility.

Protect exact source quotations, code, command flags, URLs, IDs, amounts and necessary punctuation. Do not use global em-dash/double-hyphen replacement or rewrite technical material to satisfy a style habit. If editing supplied material, preserve meaning and the user's requested scope.

## Artifact checks and honest failure

For the structured report, the [report contract](report-contract.md) and helper check field types, references, source/date statuses and score arithmetic to their stated limits. They cannot check that a website says what the author claims or whether a prospect really fits. Review those judgments separately.

Inspect final rendered files for complete sections, readable source/date labels, visible uncertainty, correct score labels, meaningful empty states and accessible layout. Verify source links and output access through available supported tools. Check the final saved version after any material edit. Do not infer browser behavior, offline operation, privacy or security from a parser pass.

If an output cannot be completed, return the supported artifact or partial result, the exact missing evidence/tool/permission and the smallest next step. Do not invent provider availability, conceal a failed source or equate a draft with a sent/published item. Review should find concrete defects, not create ritual scorecards or extra versions without purpose.
