# Interview branches

Resolve in dependency order, one to three related questions at a time. A branch closes when its acceptance test and governing scope are clear.

1. **Platform**: Claude Code, Claude Cowork, Codex, or several (several means separate scopes).
2. **Outcome**: what becomes easier, safer, faster or more consistent, and how the user will notice.
3. **People**: who operates the harness and who reviews its output.
4. **Work**: recurring tasks, deliverables, projects and source systems.
5. **Scope**: global defaults, workspace rules, project rules, exclusions.
6. **Files**: the config home or connected folders, workspaces, examples, templates and output destinations.
7. **Capabilities**: skills, plugins, connectors, MCP servers, automation surfaces and optional bundles the platform offers.
8. **Authority**: read, write, install, authenticate, publish, spend, delete and external actions.
9. **Quality**: deterministic checks, judgement criteria, reviewers and evidence surfaces.
10. **Continuity**: memory, autonomous runs, resume behavior, state files, schedules and notifications. On Cowork, where durable state lives outside the session.
11. **Maintenance**: who owns it, how drift is caught, update cadence, model-change review.

Record each answer as a fact, constraint, preference, assumption or decision.
