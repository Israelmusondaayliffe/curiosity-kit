# Research and integrate evidence

Use this method for deep, current, disputed or multi-source questions and for direct source audits. It owns the complete research procedure locally. A single authoritative lookup can return a direct answer without a permanent ledger or delegated team.

## Set the question and stopping rule

Before broad searching, identify what decision or deliverable the evidence must change. Break it into answerable questions. For each, name why it matters, the source that owns the fact, evidence that would answer it, applicable dates/population/definitions and what would disconfirm the likely answer.

Choose a bound suited to the task:

- A supplied-source task stays within those sources unless additional research is authorized.
- A bounded investigation has a named question and source/date/time/evidence limit.
- A broad field map still has excluded branches and a saturation rule.

An evidence standard can be an owning record for a factual clause, comparable observations for a quantitative comparison, or direct passages plus counterevidence for a disputed interpretation. A source quota is not an evidence standard. If no decision or deliverable would change, use a smaller lookup or reading task instead of an open-ended investigation.

Stop when the decision-relevant question is answered to the agreed standard, meaningful counterevidence has been considered and remaining gaps are named. If the bound expires first, return the strongest supported result and the precise unresolved question. Do not widen sources, dates or paid work silently.

## Route to the source of truth

Inventory relevant supplied material before public search. Respect exclusions and access restrictions. A file named in a request but absent is a missing source, not an invitation to invent its contents.

Prefer:

1. The authorized internal file or connector that owns an internal fact.
2. Primary public material: actual documentation, data, filings, standards, original research, official records or transcripts.
3. Strong secondary analysis for interpretation and context.
4. Discovery pages that lead to better evidence.

Use current sources for volatile facts. An official page may own a product claim but not independently establish its effectiveness. Evaluate source competence, incentives, publication/update dates and the fit between its measurement and the question. Do not replace a relevant internal record with generic public search because it is easier to access.

## Discover, read and record

Make an initial discovery pass to identify terminology, entities, plausible primary sources and competing explanations. Then open the actual relevant bodies. Follow a material claim through citations or linked records to its origin. A search result, citation list or tool summary does not establish that you read the underlying source.

For important evidence, use the [source and claim records](../assets/work-record-templates.md) or an equivalent compact record:

- Stable source ID, title and exact location/version.
- Owner/source type and origin chain.
- Publication/update/effective date when known, separately from actual access date.
- Fully read, relevant portion read, supplied excerpt or inaccessible, with the real extent and reason.
- Specific supporting passage, table/row or section locator.
- Relevant question/claim, definitions, population, period and limitations.

Keep unknown dates unknown. A recent access date does not make old findings current. “Supplied” identifies how material arrived, not independent proof of its authenticity or completeness. A partially read source may support the passage actually read; do not claim full-document coverage or quote a section you never opened.

For consequential searches, record the query/source scope and useful null results. “No matching record in this source/window” is different from “no record exists.” Record a failed access separately from a negative search. Do not fill the final ledger with irrelevant pages merely because they ranked highly.

## Test independence and reconcile conflict

Several documents repeating one press release, dataset, interview or author statement are one evidence chain. Trace the originating record and distinguish repetition from genuinely independent observation. Independent support is valuable where the claim warrants it, but it does not require multiple sources for a fact that one authoritative record directly owns.

For conflicting records, compare:

1. Exact claim and authority for that claim.
2. Dates, including effective date and whether a later record is a correction.
3. Definitions, units, populations, sampling and methods.
4. Source incentives and whether one is only a summary of the other.
5. What remains compatible and what cannot be resolved.

Classify the conflict as factual, methodological or interpretive. A newer authoritative amendment changes only the clause it actually supersedes. Preserve unaffected terms from the older record. For example, a revised eligibility threshold does not change an unchanged fee or deadline. A later newsletter repeating an old number does not override the owning correction.

Keep credible unresolved disagreement visible. Do not choose a source because it supports the preferred narrative, average incompatible definitions or treat reviewer agreement as new evidence. Distinguish observed behavior, a participant's stated rationale, your explanation and unknown intent. A mechanism can show how an outcome occurred without proving why a person chose it.

When historical evidence informs a plan, carry only relevant sourced constraints: what must be preserved, what changed, what prior failure to avoid and what current risk remains. Recheck whether the historical condition still applies.

## Search for what would change the answer

Once a plausible answer emerges, make a targeted counterevidence pass. Look for contrary observations, omitted populations, corrections, retractions, newer governing records and alternative explanations. Inspect accessible internal evidence that conflicts with a public claim. A tidy story is a reason to test the story, not stop early.

Then perform the saturation check. State what was answered, what did not change the conclusion, remaining uncertainty and what additional evidence could reverse the result. Do not equate search volume with confidence. Use [analyze and draft](analyze-and-draft.md) to test the claims and produce the requested complete artifact.

## Conditional parallel research

Delegate only when workers are actually available, permitted and useful, with independent questions or disjoint outputs. Finding sources, interpreting those sources and drafting from them are dependent stages. They are not three independent research lanes.

Each worker receives:

- The overall decision and its exact bounded question.
- Relevant inputs, authoritative sources, definitions and freshness rule.
- Exclusions, inherited authority, resource/stop limits and allowed writes.
- Expected result, source/claim fields and verification evidence.
- An explicit instruction to perform the assigned reading directly, without recursively delegating the same job.

Use separate output locations or returned text. One coordinator owns the combined record. Extra subdelegation needs a distinct useful task and an explicit inherited limit; do not create a circular handoff or a worker per heading. Batch independent same-shape extraction when appropriate.

Inspect each return before integration. Open decisive sources or inspect exact supplied passages, confirm actual read coverage and check whether the question was answered. A polished worker summary can still contain an unsupported claim. Returned “blocked,” partial, uncertain or failed findings must survive synthesis.

Reconcile duplicate IDs, source chains, definitions and dates. Compare cross-strand contradictions. Do not turn overlapping sources into independent votes or hide disagreement to make the final narrative smooth. Apply the same claim and artifact checks that single-agent work requires.

If workers are unavailable or unnecessary, perform the complete method sequentially. Report that mode honestly. It preserves core research; it does not create an independent reviewer or demonstrate parallel-host capability.

## Return contract and failures

Return findings that answer the original question, inspectable source locations, the relevant evidence record, counterevidence/conflicts, the stop outcome and exact gaps. For a larger task, integrate that return into the final requested artifact rather than handing the user a raw research dump.

An inaccessible essential source leaves its dependent claim unresolved. A missing optional source may permit a qualified result. Explain the distinction. Do not fabricate a replacement, imply a source was read because a related summary was available, or expand authorization to log in, buy access or contact someone. Preserve progress and identify the next evidence needed.
