# Stage 1 Skeletons

Current use: follow [previs method](../../methods/previs.md) and `validation-contract.md` for mode-specific timing, actual upload roles, autonomy and evidence. Version names and provider behavior in imported examples are historical, unverified here; reuse their craft without treating them as current native controls or rankings.
Five parameterized templates for Stage 1 storyboard image prompts. Pick by intent and brief shape.

## Skeleton 1: Canonical Director's Board

Use for: ambitious creative work, dance, narrative, character-driven shorts. Best fit for the Kōda Forest Rebirth aesthetic.

```
Create a professional film director's previsualization board.

PROJECT: [project title]
SEQUENCE: [one-line description of the motion arc]
DURATION: [N] seconds
PANELS: [panel count, e.g. 12]
GRID: [grid shape, e.g. 3x4]
ASPECT: [aspect ratio, e.g. 16:9]
TONE: [mood, e.g. mythic, kinetic, melancholic]

SUBJECT: [subject as simple stick-figure previs mannequin. Describe identity in 1-2 sentences for the hero frame.]
ENVIRONMENT: [setting, lighting, weather, key environmental elements]
ACTION ARC: [beat-by-beat motion description, one phrase per intended panel]

LAYOUT:
- Header bar with project title and sequence summary
- One large hero frame (key visual, top-left or full-width header) establishing character design, palette, and lighting
- [panel count] storyboard panels arranged in [grid shape] grid
- Footer with arrow legend, palette swatches, and audio reference notes

PER PANEL:
- Stick-figure or wireframe pose (gesture-level only, no facial detail)
- Colored motion arrows: Red=camera, Blue=subject, Yellow=light, Green=environment
- Timing bracket: [0-Xs], [Xs-Ys], etc.
- Camera grammar token: [lens, angle, distance, movement]
- SFX cue in parentheses or brackets
- Panel number (1, 2, 3...)

CAMERA GRAMMAR ACROSS SEQUENCE: [list of shot types and moves used, e.g. WS static, MS dolly-in, ECU push, CU whip-pan, MLS orbit]

PALETTE: [color description, e.g. desaturated forest greens with bioluminescent teal accents on bone-white skin]

STYLE: Low-detail previs aesthetic. Hand-drawn or schematic feel. Prioritize framing and motion clarity over rendering. No photoreal finish.

FOOTER LEGEND (must be drawn and labeled):
- Red arrow = camera movement
- Blue arrow = subject motion
- Yellow arrow = light or highlight
- Green arrow = environment response

NEGATIVE: No watermarks. No garbled text. No excessive rendering detail inside panels. Annotations stay outside panels or in clear margin labels.
```

**Worked example: Forest Rebirth (Kōda)**

```
Create a professional film director's previsualization board.

PROJECT: Forest Rebirth
SEQUENCE: A forest spirit performs an aggressive contemporary dance that triggers the surrounding forest to regrow in pulses
DURATION: 15 seconds
PANELS: 12
GRID: 3x4
ASPECT: 16:9
TONE: Mythic, kinetic, primal

SUBJECT: Bone-white humanoid spirit, sinuous limbs, no facial features. Drawn as minimal stick-figure previs mannequin per panel.
ENVIRONMENT: Ancient mossy forest at dusk. Soft volumetric god-rays through canopy. Ground responds to subject motion with bioluminescent regrowth.
ACTION ARC: 1. Spirit kneels in deep contraction. 2. Sharp arm extension upward. 3. Rapid spine arch. 4. Spinning low sweep. 5. Vertical leap. 6. Mid-air twist. 7. Hard landing into crouch. 8. Forest pulses with light response. 9. Outward arm sweep, particles rise. 10. Whip-turn into camera. 11. Open-armed pose, forest fully regrown. 12. Wide pull-back, spirit centered in restored world.

LAYOUT: Header with project title. Hero frame above the grid, separate from the twelve sequence panels. 12-panel storyboard in 3x4 grid. Footer with arrow legend and palette.

PER PANEL: Stick-figure pose, colored arrows (R=camera, B=subject, Y=light, G=environment), timing bracket, camera grammar token, SFX cue, panel number.

CAMERA GRAMMAR: WS static, MS handheld, ECU push-in, MS orbit, WS crane-up, CU whip-pan, MLS tracking, WS dolly-out.

PALETTE: Desaturated forest greens, bone-white subject, bioluminescent teal accents in environment.

STYLE: Hand-drawn previs aesthetic, low detail, schematic.

FOOTER LEGEND: Red=camera, Blue=subject, Yellow=light, Green=environment.

NEGATIVE: No watermarks, no garbled text, no photoreal rendering inside panels.
```

## Skeleton 2: Simple Panel Grid

Use for: trailers, music video shot lists, content briefs where a clean numbered grid is enough. Best fit for Qiita-style 3x3 templates.

```
Create a [panel count]-panel storyboard grid for a [duration]-second [genre] [format].

Grid: [grid shape].
Aspect: [aspect ratio].
Style: [visual style, e.g. cinematic photoreal, anime production board, ink-wash].
Consistent character design across all panels.

Panel 1: [shot type], [camera angle], [subject action], [mood]
Panel 2: [shot type], [camera angle], [subject action], [mood]
...
Panel N: [shot type], [camera angle], [subject action], [mood]

Footer: Arrow legend (Red=camera, Blue=subject, Yellow=light, Green=environment) and one-line palette description.

NEGATIVE: No watermarks. No garbled text. No annotation inside panels (keep labels in margins).
```

**Worked example: 9-panel music video trailer**

```
Create a 9-panel storyboard grid for a 15-second hip-hop music video trailer.

Grid: 3x3.
Aspect: 16:9.
Style: Anime production board with hand-drawn shot calls.
Consistent character design across all panels.

Panel 1: WS, low angle, hero standing center frame in empty white space, calm
Panel 2: MS, eye-level, hero throws first breaking move, sharp ink splash radiates
Panel 3: CU push-in, hero's hand, ink trail follows the gesture, kinetic
Panel 4: MLS, orbit, hero spins on shoulder, ink whirl around body, explosive
Panel 5: ECU, low angle, hero's foot mid-stomp, ink shockwave, impact
Panel 6: WS pull-back, hero mid-air with split pose, ink debris suspended, peak
Panel 7: MS, tracking, hero lands and freezes in B-boy pose, ink settles, control
Panel 8: CU whip-pan, hero's profile, ink dripping back into the figure, recovery
Panel 9: WS static, hero alone in white space, single ink puddle at feet, resolution

Footer: Arrow legend (Red=camera, Blue=subject, Yellow=light, Green=environment) and palette: pure white background, single-tone India ink black.

NEGATIVE: No watermarks. No garbled text. No annotation inside panels.
```

## Skeleton 3: Commercial Ad Table

Use for: product ads, brand films, anything where the client wants a tabular reference. Best fit for UISDC ZuLi-style perfume boards.

```
Generate a complete short-video storyboard design document.

TOPIC: [duration]-second [product type] advertisement for "[brand]".

LAYOUT: Clean tabular format with storyboard panels paired to metadata columns.

Columns: Shot number | Storyboard panel (visual) | Duration | Visual description | Voiceover or narration | Sound effects | Transition type

Aspect: [aspect ratio].
Style: [visual style, e.g. high-end editorial, soft-key luxury, photoreal commercial].

REQUIREMENTS:
- Ultra-high-definition rendering
- No watermarks, no garbled text
- Unified visual style throughout
- Storyboard image stays clean. Annotations live in adjacent columns.
- Product hero shot at least once in the sequence
- Final shot includes product plus logo lockup

SHOT BREAKDOWN:
Shot 1: [duration]s. [visual]. VO: "[line]". SFX: [cue]. Transition: [type].
Shot 2: [duration]s. [visual]. VO: "[line]". SFX: [cue]. Transition: [type].
...
Shot N: [duration]s. [visual]. VO: "[line]". SFX: [cue]. Transition: [type].

PALETTE: [color description].
LIGHTING: [lighting design].
```

## Skeleton 4: Fight Choreography High-Density

Use for: combat, action, paired choreography. Best fit for Atlas Cloud Ranx-style boards.

```
Design a [duration]-second fight choreography storyboard for [genre] genre.

CHARACTERS:
- Character A: [name, description, costume, weapon]
- Character B: [name, description, costume, weapon]

ENVIRONMENT: [setting] with [lighting conditions, weather, debris].

ACTION BEATS (10 to 16 distinct beats):
1. [beat, e.g. approach]
2. [beat, e.g. first strike]
3. [beat, e.g. block]
4. [beat, e.g. dodge]
5. [beat, e.g. counter]
...
N. [final beat, e.g. finishing pose or reversal]

OUTPUT: One composite image with [panel count] panels, chosen for the duration and choreographic detail; preserve an explicit user count.

PER PANEL:
- Stick-figure poses for both characters in their relative positions
- Motion arrows: Red=camera, Blue=Character A subject motion, Purple=Character B subject motion, Yellow=light, Green=environment or debris
- Timing bracket
- Camera shot type and movement
- SFX marker (impact, whoosh, crack, footstep, etc.)
- Panel number

Aspect: [aspect ratio].

STYLE: Hand-drawn storyboard feel, rough but readable. Fight diagram clarity prioritized over render polish.

FOOTER:
- Arrow legend (full, including per-character colors)
- Character reference column on the right side: front view, side view, weapon detail for both A and B
- Environment overhead map (small inset, top-down view of the fight space with character positions per major beat)

NEGATIVE: No watermarks, no garbled text, no photoreal rendering inside panels.
```

## Skeleton 5: Character-Driven Narrative

Use for: shorts with a named protagonist, narrative arcs with reversals, character-led stories. Best fit for UISDC Wuxia and Nordic Warrior boards.

```
Create a film director's storyboard design board.

PROJECT: [title]
PROTAGONIST: [name] — [description, age, key visual identifiers, costume].
SECONDARY CHARACTERS (if any): [list with brief descriptions].

NARRATIVE ARC: [one-paragraph story with reversal or emotional turn].

SHOT LIST ([panel count] numbered shots):
1. [shot description] — [shot type], [camera move], [emotion or beat label]
2. [shot description] — [shot type], [camera move], [emotion or beat label]
...
N. [shot description] — [shot type], [camera move], [emotion or beat label]

Duration: [N] seconds. Aspect: [aspect].
Style: [visual style], 4K cinematic feel inside panels, but storyboard remains low-detail previs.

INCLUDE CHARACTER REFERENCE SHEET (right-side column):
- Protagonist: full body front, side, three-quarter back, facial expression set (neutral, intensity, exertion, resolution), costume detail close-ups.
- Each secondary character: front, side, key prop or weapon.

INCLUDE OVERHEAD CAMERA MAP (small inset, top-down view): character starting positions, key movement paths through the sequence space.

FOOTER:
- Arrow legend (R=camera, B=subject, Y=light, G=environment)
- Palette swatches
- Audio reference: one-line mood or score description

PER PANEL:
- Stick-figure pose with rough costume silhouette
- Motion arrows (color-coded)
- Timing bracket
- Camera grammar token
- SFX cue
- Beat label (emotion or narrative function)
- Panel number

NEGATIVE: No watermarks, no garbled text, no photoreal finish inside panels.
```

**Worked example: Nordic Warrior vs Dragon**

```
Create a film director's storyboard design board.

PROJECT: Last Light of the North
PROTAGONIST: Sigrid — late 20s, broad-shouldered, fur-and-leather armor with rune-etched chest plate, long braided hair, rune axe in right hand.
SECONDARY: Frostfire Wyrm — ancient dragon, ice-cracked scales, eyes the color of glacier core.

NARRATIVE ARC: Sigrid walks across a frozen battlefield ruin toward a perched dragon. The dragon attacks. She dodges, counters, and ignites her rune axe blue. The dragon hesitates. Sigrid steps forward.

SHOT LIST (8 shots over 15 seconds):
1. WS, static, Sigrid back-to-camera walking into the frozen ruin. Dragon a distant silhouette on the ice cliff. — wide static, mood: ominous resolve.
2. MS, slow push-in, Sigrid lifts her head into the wind, braid and furs whip back. — medium push, mood: decisive.
3. ELS, fast tracking, dragon dives breathing fire across the snow. — wide tracking, mood: tension.
4. MS, handheld, Sigrid rolls right, axe scrapes ice. — medium handheld, mood: reaction.
5. MCU, low angle, Sigrid rises, axe ignites blue runefire. — close low, mood: ignition.
6. WS, crane down, dragon banks hard, hesitating mid-air. — wide crane, mood: doubt.
7. MS, tracking forward, Sigrid steps into the dragon's shadow. — medium tracking, mood: control.
8. ECU, push, Sigrid's eyes, runefire reflected. — extreme close push, mood: held resolve.

Duration: 15 seconds. Aspect: 16:9.
Style: Norse mythic, cold blue and slate palette, photoreal cinematic inside hero frame, low-detail previs inside panels.

CHARACTER REFERENCE SHEET (right column): Sigrid front, side, back, expression set, axe detail. Dragon side view, head close-up, scale texture sample.

OVERHEAD MAP: top-down of the ruin, Sigrid's path from frame-left to center, dragon's flight arc from cliff to dive.

FOOTER: Arrow legend, palette swatches, audio reference: low brass and distant wind, single tonal hit at panel 5.

PER PANEL: stick-figure pose, colored arrows, timing bracket, camera token, SFX cue, beat label, panel number.

NEGATIVE: No watermarks, no garbled text, no photoreal inside panels.
```

## Skeleton selection guide

| Brief characteristic | Use skeleton |
|---|---|
| Dance, movement-led art, single subject | 1 (Director's Board) |
| Music video shot list, simple trailer | 2 (Simple Grid) |
| Brand or product commercial | 3 (Commercial Table) |
| Combat, paired choreography, action | 4 (Fight Choreography) |
| Named protagonist, narrative arc, reversal | 5 (Character-Driven Narrative) |

If the brief spans two categories (e.g. character-driven action), default to the more complex skeleton (4 or 5) and trim what is not needed.

## Standalone JSON form

When JSON is requested, preserve the chosen skeleton's shot detail in a structured brief. JSON organizes instructions; it is not a provider control schema. Fill every field and preserve actual reference roles. Use natural-language values, and omit timing/audio fields when the selected format does not call for them.

```json
{
  "task": "Create one composite storyboard image",
  "project": "[title]",
  "duration_seconds": "[requested duration]",
  "panel_count": "[sequence panel count, excluding hero inset and sheets]",
  "aspect_ratio": "[requested ratio]",
  "references": [{"image": "[actual image label]", "role": "[identity, style, environment, or hero]"}],
  "subject": "[identity or product/world description]",
  "environment": "[setting and response to action]",
  "palette": "[colors and their relationships]",
  "layout": "[selected director board, grid, table, fight or narrative layout]",
  "panels": [{"id": 1, "timing": "[start-end seconds, if timed]", "action": "[specific gesture and choreography]", "camera": "[lens, angle, shot and move]", "sound": "[only if requested]"}],
  "arrow_legend": {"red": "camera", "blue": "subject", "yellow": "light", "green": "environment"},
  "style": "[requested rendering treatment]",
  "preserve": "[identity, world and continuity choices]",
  "exclude": "[unwanted artifacts; retain intentional text]"
}
```

For a system-style brief, use the same complete content grouped under PURPOSE, REFERENCES, LAYOUT, PER-PANEL PLAN, CONTINUITY and OUTPUT. Keep it an instruction to the image model, without claiming hidden orchestration or generation controls.
