## Scene and camera construction
Use positive scene, action and camera descriptions. Put an important camera movement early or late rather than burying it in unrelated detail. Give the shot one observable action with a feasible ending. For storyboard mode, author one prompt per six-second interval and re-establish subject/location at each interval. A long single-prompt generation is a different mode; a numbered storyboard in prose does not automatically select it.

Authored six-second interval: “Slow dolly toward a weathered wooden cabin beside a still lake at dawn. Mist drifts across the water as a warm light turns on in the cabin window.”
Next interval, if requested: “From the same lakeside position, the camera holds on the lit cabin. A person opens the door and steps onto the porch.” Pass each through the actual storyboard input for the selected version. Do not promise exact camera paths, native audio or identity continuity without supported controls and output review.
