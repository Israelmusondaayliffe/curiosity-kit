## Route the requested change
Separate object/background removal or replacement, segmentation masks, green-screen output and upscaling. A mask identifies the edited region; an appearance prompt describes a replacement. Neither substitutes for the other. Streaming and finite-file transports have different contracts and must not share guessed payloads.

Authored editing intent: “Replace the wall behind the speaker with a plain warm-grey studio background. Preserve the speaker, clothing, gestures, timing and foreground desk.” This is an operation brief only.
