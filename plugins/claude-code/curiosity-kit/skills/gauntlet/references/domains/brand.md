# Brand

## Unit and bar

Choose a positioning line, identity element, asset family, or concrete channel deployment. Split an undefined brand refresh into inspectable outcomes. Use real reference identity systems for craft, plus project-specific hard constraints for integrity. A reference's palette does not override the approved brief.

Declare required export sizes, formats, font or token rules, and relevant accessibility checks. A palette restriction applies only when requested. Freeze its exact measurement method and threshold before work starts; do not import another project's preferences.

## Inspection recipe

Render visual elements at every declared use size, including the smallest meaningful deployment. Render every member of an asset family and inspect the family together. Capture actual commands, outputs, and exit codes with `python3 scripts/inspection.py check --input payload.json`. Source SVG or CSS inspection cannot substitute for pixels.

Run scripted constraint checks where possible, such as declared palette tolerance, token conformance, or contrast requirements. Keep their pass/fail results separate from aesthetic judgment.

For positioning and text-bearing assets, a fresh reader answers: Who is this for? What does this person or organization do? What would I click next? Record locations and guesses, then validate the frozen question record with `python3 scripts/inspection.py reader --input payload.json`. Trace factual positioning claims with the claims command.

## Comparison and worked check

Blind-pair matched rendered elements or whole asset sets. Seal provenance and strip removable metadata. If a mandatory monochrome design is recognizable against colorful references, disclose partial blindness and judge hierarchy, type, spacing, and composition without treating the constraint as a taste defect.

A wordmark that works only in a large presentation fails its small-size use case. A carousel family can fail consistency even when each frame looks polished alone. Verify no placeholders, missing references, unsupported claims, or unauthorized constraint exceptions survive. Use strategy for choosing the positioning, research for audience evidence, and visual for interaction-heavy product layouts.
