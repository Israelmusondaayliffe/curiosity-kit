# Assets, geography and performance

## Keep identity separate from the shot

Give each reusable entity a stable identity and each materially different appearance or condition its own asset ID and tag. Retain its parent identity. A coat becoming wet, a broken tool, or a concealed prop must not silently replace the earlier state. A crowd can be one asset only when its exact count and collective role are declared. A new state propagates only through affected shots.

Write a concise descriptor of durable face/body/material/state features. Preserve approved descriptor text at compile time. Keep scene camera, colour grade and action elsewhere. Record each reference's permitted influence and excluded influence. An identity reference must not dictate composition; a location reference must not smuggle in its camera angle or colour treatment.

### Character proof

Design a neutral three-view sheet: a prominent three-quarter face with readable eyes; a front body view with facial information excluded to avoid competing face sources; and a back view. Use neutral background and controllable lighting. Keep skin/material detail and eye reflections readable. Do not bake grain, grade or dramatic lens distortion into the master.

Plan a ten-condition identity test across pose, distance, lighting and shared scenes, including the actual intended light. The strict identity bar is recognition in all ten conditions; report how many were actually inspected. With no generated material, this is a proof plan, never ten claimed passes. On failure, inspect the descriptor, reference choice and contradictory states before blaming a model or buying more attempts.

Preserve the approved master. For a local defect, propose a small edit and masked/composited return to that master when supported. Avoid repeatedly regenerating the whole approved image. If compositing tools are absent, describe the needed operation and stop short of claiming it occurred. Authorize a new full pass only as a new candidate, with identity rechecked.

### Locations and props

Design a three-quarter location view with foreground, midground, background, one visible anchor and one motivated main light. Locate entrances, routes and playable corners. Make two reverse-angle plans: a viewpoint derived from the locked map, or a separately built reverse reference checked against that map. Neither proves unseen geometry without inspection.

The scene geography includes landmark-relative positions, distances in metres when known, frame-left/frame-right, the camera's side of the action axis, a crossing rule and a reverse-angle rule. Every independent shot restates it. Restrict intimate dialogue to the usable corner instead of describing an entire building.

Props need scale, material, state, readable detail, holder/contact and visibility. Give a transfer one owner before and one after; show the giver releasing it. Hidden, open, damaged or emptied states get separate tags when the image would differ. For unusually large entities, use a visible scale anchor rather than an adjective.

## Output and repair

Return an actual character/location/prop prompt or asset sheet using the form in [prompt modes](../references/prompt-modes.md), plus its reference map and observed or pending proof. A failed stress test returns to the descriptor/reference or state that caused it. For performance, load [acting](acting.md).
