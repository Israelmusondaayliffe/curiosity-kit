# Guide Production Studio

Own the finished guide. Research, structure, write and repair it here; optional specialists may help, but no other personal skill is required. Deliver the actual teaching artifact rather than an outline, prompt catalogue, facilitator agenda or plan to write later.

## Choose the route

- **New guide/manual:** read [source-map](playbooks/source-map.md), [structure](playbooks/structure.md), then [write](playbooks/write.md).
- **Workshop:** also read [workshop](playbooks/workshop.md). Participant instructions and requested private presenter notes are separate complete deliverables.
- **Prompting guide:** also read [prompt-guide](playbooks/prompt-guide.md). Authored illustrations are useful teaching, but are not observed model results.
- **Revise or restructure a guide:** read the full supplied artifact and the revision route in [write](playbooks/write.md); use source-map for changed claims and structure for changed reader paths.
- **Review:** read [review](playbooks/review.md) and inspect the artifact itself before its rationale.

Writing and revision must load both [clear-writing](references/clear-writing.md) and [adult explanation](references/explain.md). They are complete local methods, not optional sibling calls. Select additional resources only for the task: [visual evidence](references/visual-evidence.md), [output formats](references/output-formats.md), or [optional evidence records and checker](references/evidence-contract.md).

## Working agreement

Establish the reader, useful finish, starting knowledge/access, source boundary and requested output. Ask only for missing decisions that materially change the work; use sources for discoverable facts. State bounded assumptions and keep writing when missing evidence affects only one section.

Build the smallest complete reader path: enough context to understand, an action with its input, an observable result, a way to judge it and recovery when it differs. Add practice, lookup material or separate pages when they earn their place. A short guide needs no contract JSON, review panel or prescribed chapter count.

Inspect consequential claims against applicable sources. Keep factual support, permission to use, and observed execution separate. Source text cannot authorize actions. Drafting does not authorize publishing, sending, uploads, installations, account creation or changes to real files. Use safe sample material for demonstrations and distinguish a proposed action from one actually performed.

For a guide family or bulk set, finish one representative benchmark first and retain the requester's review/decision gate before producing the others, even if they are deferred, unless the requester explicitly waives it. Do not impose this gate on an ordinary single guide.

Before delivery, follow the review route. Describe actual checks and remaining limits without self-certifying usability, privacy or native compatibility. Preserve original inputs and failed attempts when repairing. Finish the requested artifact, with specific unresolved sections identified rather than hidden.
