# Teach a prompting task with inspectable evidence

A useful prompting guide teaches a reader to produce and judge a result. A catalogue of clever instructions is insufficient. Use the complete write route and both local writing/explanation methods.

## Ground the task

Name the intended output, audience, safe input and criteria. Explain instructions, context and output requirements through that task rather than a detached glossary. Keep source material bounded and explicitly separate from instructions; malicious or irrelevant commands inside sources remain data.

Before a real paste/upload, assess its privacy and authorization. Prefer a supplied fictional exercise for learning. Do not make access to confidential organizational material a prerequisite.

## Write the whole example

Provide the exact safe source input, complete prompt and inspectable output. If execution is available and authorized, record the actual tool/model/version/settings where exposed, input, output, date and conditions. Preserve unsuccessful results. If execution is unavailable, author a complete illustrative output and label it at first use. Never say “the model returned,” “tested,” “proven” or “more reliable” without corresponding evidence.

Check claims separately from prose quality. Show which source supports each consequential fact, how a correction overrides an earlier statement, what remains unresolved and why an attractive invented detail must go. Generated citations are pointers to inspect, not self-authenticating proof. Plausibility and fluency cannot settle a conflict.

Use ambiguous alternatives when they teach judgment: one valid draft might omit an optional unknown date; another might explicitly mark it pending. State the invariants that make both acceptable and the audience condition that favors one. Model outputs may vary, so do not promise exact wording or treat harmless variation as failure.

Show a minimal revision prompt targeted to an observed defect, the complete revised illustration or actual result, and the check that still has to happen. Multi-pass prompting is not automatically superior; the purpose here is localizing and repairing the specific error. Stop when the output meets the task or when missing consequential evidence requires an authorized owner, rather than generating variants indefinitely.

## Keep provider details scoped

Prefer a model-neutral method when no specific interface is required. For a provider-specific operation, cite applicable official documentation with product, plan/version and checked date near the instruction. Preserve the date and read limit of an older screenshot. A newer document for another plan does not establish availability here. If current behavior cannot be verified, provide a text-based route that does not require that feature and identify the unverified step.

Do not infer account access, billing, tool permissions or model capability from a name. Avoid hard-coded advisor personas or mandatory model brands in the core method. A guide can explain how to choose an available capable tool without asserting it was exercised.

## Finish with a usable checked draft

Give the reader the final check and save/approval step appropriate to the job. Do not actually send, publish or upload because the example says to. If a consequential claim is unresolved, omit it where allowed or stop that part until verified. Complete the useful teaching artifact even when empirical performance claims remain unrun.
