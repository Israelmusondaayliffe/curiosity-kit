## Spatial reasoning without imagined extra outputs

Authored Uni 1.1 reference-fusion prompt: “Use the chair from the first reference, the wall material from the second, and the camera angle from the third. Place the chair close to the wall so its feet contact the floor. Preserve the chair's tubular frame and upholstery; borrow no other furniture.” Keep each reference role separate from the API input list.

For a camera study: “Keep the room, chair position and lighting fixed; show the same moment from a low camera beside the front-right leg.” For a new moment: “The chair has been pulled away from the wall; retain its design and the same room.” These are different transformations, not interchangeable angles.

Retain historical Uni-1 source recipes, including the NL/JSON eight-template library, as explicitly historical authoring targets. Structured JSON can express the same brief inside prompt; it does not become a native seed or negative schema. A four-panel storyboard requests one composition unless actual multiple-file delivery is separately supported and requested.
