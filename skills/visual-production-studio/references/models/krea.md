## Explore, then constrain deliberately
For an open-ended exploration, start broad enough to expose different looks. Once a direction is chosen, give a concrete subject and lock its important shape, lettering or arrangement. Style references and independently usable moodboards are distinct control paths with different interpretation and capacity; keep the selected one explicit. Increasing reference strength can overpower subject detail, so inspect the hard invariants after each change.

Authored refinement prompt: “A compact red portable radio on a pale yellow shelf, straight-on framing, rounded rectangular housing, two cream dials and a black speaker grille. Warm late-afternoon light.” Borrow only the approved palette/texture from the style reference; if its strength erases the radio's dial layout, reduce or revise that influence on the chosen UI rather than padding the prompt with contradictory style words.
