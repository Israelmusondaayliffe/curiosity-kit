# Developmental editing

Read the complete draft, intended use and relevant context before polishing sentences. Evaluate whether the piece works for its reader: argument or exploration, structure, evidence, stakes, opening promise and payoff. An essay need not prove a business thesis; a how-to need not manufacture personal stakes.

Inspect each meaningful section. Does its contribution earn its place? Does order suit the material? Are logical steps and needed context present? What backs the claim at its actual scope? Does a first-time reader need trust or knowledge the piece has not earned? Does the ending deliver what the opening promised?

## Structural tests

Subsection summary: write one sentence describing what each subsection establishes. Compare its paragraphs, examples and asides against that contribution. Flag apparently disconnected material, then check whether it supplies evidence, context, voice or a valuable counterpoint. Only cut/relocate if doing so improves the piece; an orphaned good line may belong elsewhere, not in the trash. Return summaries alongside significant findings when useful.

Twenty-second pitch: describe the actual whole piece briefly, then compare it with the introduction's thesis or promise. A gap may mean the introduction drifted, the body changed direction or the promise is underdelivered. Do not assume which must change. Offer the smallest supported repair.

## Evidence and scope

A lived experience, named example, expert quotation, study or dataset is useful only for what it establishes. Inspect the source, relevant date, population and limitation when available. Distinguish evidence from the writer's interpretation. Anonymous “studies show,” unestablished authority, cherry-picked examples and confident unsupported assertions deserve located findings. If sources are absent, ask or mark support unverified rather than accusing the writer of fabrication. Use [claim checking](business-and-claims.md) for a material ledger.

## Output and action

Prioritize critical problems that prevent the piece working, consequential improvements worth considering, then minor polish. No quota. Quote the passage and explain its consequence for this audience. A short piece can receive a brief assessment; comprehensive review may follow section order. State whether structure is ready for sentence work, needs a targeted pass or needs significant restructuring.

Diagnose-only returns findings without changing text. An authorized revision can directly repair supported issues; ask only about a consequential change in position, meaning or missing personal material. Preserve valuable irregularity and unresolved questions. Do not force the writer to accept every suggestion or invent a conclusion to close a report.

The method returns to its parent. It does not invoke Scribe again, start independent reviewers, save lessons or authorize publication. Expanded named lenses belong only to an explicit request.
