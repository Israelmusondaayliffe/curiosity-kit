# Write the requested piece

Own the accepted writing outcome from useful context through delivery. This is the local Scribe function, not a requirement to march through every method.

1. Identify the artifact, audience, intended use, operation, requested length/format, available sources and actual writer. Read [context](../references/context.md) and relevant [channel guidance](../references/channels.md). Use supplied writing immediately. No scaffold, saved guide, business decision or complete profile is a prerequisite.
2. If material intent is missing, use [interview](interview.md) to elicit the writer's account. A complete brief proceeds directly. Ask about a consequential thesis change or unsupported personal fact, not routine editing choices. For open exploration use [brainstorm](brainstorm.md); for developed notes choose an appropriately light [outline](outline.md).
3. Write the complete requested artifact using [draft](draft.md). An outline is not a delivered article; a list of ideas is not a finished post. Give one result unless the user asked for alternatives. Match a requested selection or section rather than expanding it to a whole piece.
4. Select the smallest useful repair: [developmental edit](developmental-edit.md) for argument/structure; [business and claims](business-and-claims.md) for purpose/support; [line edit](line-edit.md) for sentences, simplification or revision residue; [craft](craft.md) for a particular opening, thesis, promise, bridge or analogy; [BLUF](bluf.md) for importance/placement; [voice check](voice-check.md) for drift. These methods preserve diagnose/propose/revise/readiness scope and never launch reviewers themselves.
5. Apply [Unslop](unslop.md) to actual defects, beginning with provenance and certainty before vocabulary. Do not make the writer less themselves to satisfy a score. Do not invent a scene, data, source, memory, opinion or quirky detail to make writing vivid.
6. Follow the [two-reviewer contract](../references/review.md) for complete deliverables. Freeze the same draft for both independent reviewers, integrate concrete findings once, and perform at most one focused recheck of an actual repair. A failed reviewer is unavailable, not approval. A user's skipped review still leaves the writer responsible for factual care and protected text.
7. Use [final pass](final-pass.md), then one bounded Unslop DELIVERY check of the resulting version. Deliver the artifact first. Explain only material uncertainties, requested changes or unavailable required checks. Do not add a scorecard, sales pitch, hashtags, extra variants or another-stage offer by habit.
8. If new durable learning is supported, [propose a guide update](guides.md) with source cue and destination. Save only after confirmation. No lesson is required, no background learning occurs, and publishing/sending are separate actions.

Named creative lenses, hostile/supportive personas, aggressive economy, expanded panels/debates and corpus analysis require affirmative selection. A generic “make this better” does not authorize them. Reuse the active piece folder and workspace convention; do not create a parallel work system.
