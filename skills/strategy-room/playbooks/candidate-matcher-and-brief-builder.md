# Candidate Matcher and Brief Builder

Use when explicitly selected or routed to match a bounded outcome to a person, agent, tool, product or service. The decision is who/what fits the job. Plugin ownership/discovery/installation belongs to Capability Operator, and broad strategic choices belong to other Strategy Room methods. Read the [common evidence/choice contract](../references/evidence-and-choice.md); use the [match and brief template](../assets/candidate-match.md).

## Define the job before the favorite

Record outcome, audience, deliverable, must-have capabilities, preferences, exclusions, timing, supplied budget, collaboration needs, permissions and acceptance evidence. Separate disqualifying gates from comparative preferences. If the outcome is too vague to judge fit, ask the smallest defining question rather than rank familiar names.

Preserve user-supplied candidates unless clearly ineligible. Add candidates only when the set is insufficient and current research is in scope. Inspect current public professional/product evidence for claims whose drift matters: features, demonstrated work, compatibility, geography, cost or stated availability. For people use relevant professional information and respect high-impact decision rules; never infer sensitive traits, private circumstances, willingness, capacity or availability. An old public price or portfolio is not a current quote or commitment.

## Qualify and compare

Apply every must-have to every candidate as pass/fail/unknown with evidence. A hard failure cannot be averaged away by strengths elsewhere. Unknown compatibility, cost or capacity stays unknown. Compare qualifying/provisional candidates against the same visible criteria and evidence bar. For finalists show strongest fit evidence, gaps, tradeoffs and disqualifiers; avoid precision unsupported by the sources.

Return exactly one overall status:
- **qualified match**: one recommended candidate plus a ranked shortlist when alternatives exist, with required gates established.
- **provisional match**: a lead candidate with a named unknown that could still disqualify it; state the exact check needed.
- **no qualified match**: explain failed must-haves and the smallest next search, constraint decision or fallback.

A famous candidate does not beat demonstrated task fit. Do not force a match or silently relax budget/privacy constraints. If a user chooses a candidate despite a failed gate, expose that conflict and ask whether the requirement changed before presenting a qualified result.

## Make the handoff usable

For a qualified match, write a complete brief: outcome/audience, scope and exclusions, supplied inputs and source locations, deliverable format, responsibilities and boundaries, acceptance evidence, timing/budget as actually known, dependencies, risks/open questions and next owner/action. No invented commercial terms or commitments.

A provisional match gets a clearly non-authorizing draft with the unresolved qualification condition up front. No-match gets a search/fallback brief rather than a fictitious assignee. Inspect completeness and artifact links. Return evidence-based recommendation, status and appropriate brief. Sending it, contacting a person, assigning work, buying, installing or changing permissions requires separate authority.
