# Strategy Room

Make the next consequential choice understandable, evidence-grounded and owned by the person entitled to make it. Work in business, creative, technical or personal contexts without turning every request into a strategy exercise.

## Select the operation

Identify the actual decision, owner, horizon, constraints, stakes/reversibility and available evidence from the request. Ask only for a material missing input. Honor explicit method selection; quoted, negated or historical names do not activate it. One or two ordinary clarifications do not require an interview. Already accepted execution does not require reopening the decision; return the accepted direction and its limits to the requested execution workflow.

Read **one** selected playbook. Each is complete and names its required resources; load further references only at the relevant phase.

- **Interview**: [Grill Me](playbooks/grill-me.md), explicit full interview to resolve material ambiguity.
- **Wayfind**: [Decision Wayfinder](playbooks/decision-wayfinder.md), explicit uncertain-effort map, frontier and next blocking decision.
- **Challenge**: [Assumption Challenger](playbooks/assumption-challenger.md), researched or supplied-evidence scrutiny of a premise/plan. Ordinary critique belongs here rather than silently activating Grill Me.
- **Explore**: [Multi-Direction Explorer](playbooks/multi-direction-explorer.md), meaningful alternatives or narrowing an existing set.
- **Synthesize**: [Decision Synthesizer](playbooks/decision-synthesizer.md), mature evidence/options needing a sufficiency verdict and choice gate.
- **Register**: [Assumption Register](playbooks/assumption-register.md), durable load-bearing beliefs and changes in evidence.
- **Prioritize**: [Curiosity Compass](playbooks/curiosity-compass.md), rough ideas competing for attention, zero to three viable directions.
- **Match**: [Candidate Matcher and Brief Builder](playbooks/candidate-matcher-and-brief-builder.md), who/what fits a bounded job.
- **Productize**: [Proof-to-Product Mapper](playbooks/proof-to-product-mapper.md), reusable form for observed work and the cheapest next validation.

A request may explicitly combine methods. Complete the first needed output and carry only its relevant artifact into the next; do not preload nine playbooks. If the decision is unclear, state the missing decision rather than running a generic brainstorm. Return an ordinary concise answer when that is sufficient. A saved structured route is optional; its exact contract and checker are in [contracts](assets/contracts/README.md).

## Work and return

Use the selected method's local steps and [evidence/choice contract](references/evidence-and-choice.md). No sibling skill, provider, API, account, model name, database or worker is required for core reasoning. Current factual research uses available authorized tools; supplied-only work stays supplied-only. Preserve material unknowns when evidence or tools are unavailable.

Return the requested usable artifact or answer, its governing evidence, unresolved material limits and the next decision/owner. If you saved files, inspect their contents and include working clickable links to the actual delivered artifacts in the final answer; saying a packet exists is not a handoff. A recommendation, draft brief or accepted understanding does not itself authorize external action or implementation. Check substantive correctness and boundaries before returning; structural helper success is only one part of that check.

Optional neighbors: Harness Engineering owns setup-specific harness interview/build; Outcome Engine can execute an accepted direction; Continuity Vault can preserve a map; Capability Operator owns plugin discovery/governance. Strategy Room works without them. Explicit Matt method requests retain their named ownership rather than being silently replaced here.
