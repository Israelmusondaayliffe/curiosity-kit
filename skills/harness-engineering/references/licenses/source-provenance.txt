# Source provenance

Writing Enforcer is the canonical owner of the merged private writing capability.

## Merged sources

- `Unslop Skill`, user-owned private source, inspected on 2026-08-26. Its instructions were integrated with a source-backed voice boundary. Private source identifiers stay outside the distributable plugin.
- `humanizer`, copyright 2025 Siqi Chen, MIT, from `blader/humanizer`. Its taxonomy, voice tools, and source-preservation guidance were already substantially present in Writing Enforcer. The carried local snapshot did not record an exact upstream revision, so no revision is claimed.
- `stop-slop`, copyright 2025 Hardik Pandya, MIT. Its reader-distance, false-agency, density, and directness checks were merged selectively.
- `no-ai-slop`, copyright 2026 Peter Yang, MIT, reviewed at commit `61c21c351da4dcb40946a11fead978f2078a2c65`. It supplied minimum-effective editing, detect-only behavior, structural patterns, and the no-invented-personality boundary.
- `avoid-ai-writing`, copyright 2026 Conor Bronsdon, MIT. It remains a cited source for the extended pattern catalog.
- `Wikipedia:Signs of AI writing`, by its Wikipedia contributors, revision `1371562925`, CC BY-SA 4.0. The local pattern taxonomy adapts and extends its categories for production writing.

The retired loose folders remain in the dated rollback backup recorded by the consolidation receipt. They are not independent active routes after successful release.
